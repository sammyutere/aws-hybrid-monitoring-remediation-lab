Recovery classification (Day 23):

Operational health:
- up{job="aws-node"} confirms current reachability

Statistical recovery:
- job:aws_node:up:ratio_5m shows recent availability window
- job:aws_node:burn_rate_5m shows recent error budget consumption

Alert interpretation:
- Active alerts after recovery may reflect historical downtime within evaluation windows
- Not all alerts are expected to clear immediately after service restoration

Interpretation guide:
- up = 1 → service is operationally healthy
- ratio_5m < 1 → recent downtime still affects alert logic
- burn_rate_5m > 0 → recent errors still impacting SLO calculations
- long-window alerts (1h / 24h) may remain firing even after recovery

Key insight:
Prometheus alerts are time-window based, not instantaneous.

A recovered service can still produce active alerts if the evaluation window
includes earlier downtime.

This is expected behavior, not a failure of the recovery process.
