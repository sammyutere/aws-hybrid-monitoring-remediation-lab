Day 28 recovery interpretation:

This lab validates automated restore readiness.

Operational recovery means:
- backup archive exists
- checksum verifies successfully
- restored files exist
- restored Compose config validates
- current monitoring stack remains healthy

Statistical recovery means:
- long-window alerts have decayed and cleared

If long-window SLO or burn-rate alerts are still present, they may reflect historical downtime and not a failure of the restore validation.
