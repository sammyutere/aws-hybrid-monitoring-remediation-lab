Day 29 recovery interpretation:

This lab validates evidence packaging and audit readiness.

Operational success means:
- evidence bundle is created
- checksum verifies
- archive contents can be listed
- archive can be extracted
- included Compose config validates
- monitoring stack remains healthy

Statistical recovery means:
- long-window SLO and burn-rate alerts have decayed and cleared

If long-window alerts are still visible, they may reflect historical downtime and not a failure of the Day 29 packaging process.
